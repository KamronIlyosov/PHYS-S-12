Week 02 · 2D Design

2D Design and Cutting

First time on the laser cutter, and my first run at Fusion 360.

The box

This was my first time using the laser cutter. The assignment was to make a small box out of a single flat sheet of cardboard, so the box had to be designed in 2D and then folded / glued together once the laser was done. The 6 sides of a box don't actually fit on a flat sheet in a single rectangle, so the design has all 6 panels laid out flat with finger joints on the edges, which is the rectangular tab pattern you see on the corners. When you fold them up the tabs interlock and the box stays together. I added my name on one of the side panels so it would get engraved during the cut instead of cut all the way through.

Once the file was ready I sent it to the laser, set the power and speed (lower power for engraving, full power for the through cuts), and the whole thing took maybe a minute and a half to cut. After that it was just folding the panels along the score lines and pressing the finger joints together. The fit was tight enough that I didn't even need glue.

Assignment 2: Fusion 360 Tutorial

Next was learning Fusion 360. I followed along with the tutorial video and modeled a Lego brick, which is actually a good first model because it has a flat base, the studs on top, and the hollow tubes underneath that the studs from the brick below grip onto. So you get practice with sketches, extrudes, circular patterns, and shelling, all in one part. Mine isn't perfect but it does look like a Lego brick.

Assignment 3: Fusion Modeling

For the next part of the assignment we had to model 2 household objects in Fusion 360 and put them into an assembly. I picked an iPhone and a portable MagSafe charger, because both are pretty simple shapes (mostly rectangles and rounded corners), and because together they make an obvious assembly, the charger snaps onto the back of the phone.

The phone is a rounded rectangle extruded out to about 8mm, with a smaller rectangle on the back for the camera bump and 2 circles for the lenses. The MagSafe is a flat disc with a thinner inset on top and a cable coming off the side. Once both were modeled I made an assembly file, imported both bodies as components, and used a joint to lock the charger to the back of the phone like it would actually snap on in real life. This was my first time doing an assembly in Fusion so it took longer than the parts themselves did.

The assembly

What I took away

The biggest thing I learned this week was that the laser cutter is easier than it looks once you understand that everything is 2D vector paths, and that Fusion 360 is harder than it looks because the constraint system fights you until you understand sketches. Both are going to come up a lot in the next few weeks, so it was good to get the basics out of the way early.
